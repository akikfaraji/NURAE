# NURAE Refund Policy

**Version:** [VERSION] · **Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Operator:** [REGISTERED NAME] ("NURAE", "we") · **Contact:** [SUPPORT EMAIL]

This policy forms part of the Terms of Service and explains refunds for **Credits**, **plans** and other payments. Nothing here excludes mandatory rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country.

## 1. The short version

| What | Refundable? |
|---|---|
| Unused **Credits from a Stars top-up** | Yes, at our discretion — usually reversed as a Stars refund via Telegram |
| Unused **Credits from a crypto top-up** | **No** — blockchain transfers are irreversible; we may (rarely, at discretion) grant a goodwill credit instead |
| **Credits already consumed** (messages sent, AI turns, builder rounds, hosting days, broadcasts, files) | No |
| **Plan period already started** (Plus/Pro) | No — the period runs to its end; no auto-renew is active, so nothing renews unless you buy again |
| **Free daily allowances / trial / referral premium days** | Nothing paid — nothing to refund |
| **Bot hosting days** | No refund once a bot has been hosted for the day |

## 2. Stars top-ups

- Telegram processes Stars purchases; Telegram's own rules and tax treatment apply to the purchase itself.
- If you believe a top-up was a mistake or unauthorized: contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] within **14 days** of the charge with your order number (format `T…`, shown in Billing).
- Where justified, we may refund via Telegram's payment-refund mechanism (`refundStarPayment`). Refunded Stars return through Telegram; timing is Telegram's.
- We will decline refunds where the Credits were already consumed, where the account shows abuse/fraud signals, or where the refund would breach Telegram's rules.
- We may decline and instead close the account where we suspect fraud; suspected unauthorized use should also be reported to Telegram.

## 3. Crypto top-ups

- Crypto payments are **final and non-refundable**. We cannot reverse blockchain transfers.
- Always verify the asset, network and address shown in the order. Funds sent to a wrong address, wrong network, or after the order window may be permanently lost; we will try to help where technically possible but cannot guarantee recovery.
- Where a crypto payment cannot be credited (wrong amount, unreadable hash, order expired), we may, at our discretion: credit a corrected order, hold the value as goodwill credit, or return the equivalent minus network fees to the sending address where feasible.
- Orders above our configured caps require manual review and may be rejected and returned minus network fees.

## 4. Plans

- Plans are 30-day periods bought from wallet credit. Once a period starts, it is **not refundable**, but it runs to its full end and its benefits (allowances multiplier, included hosting) remain active.
- Renewals currently never happen automatically; check your plan status before buying so you don't stack a renewal you didn't intend. (If we later introduce auto-renew, it will be opt-in and governed by the then-current policy.)
- If a plan's core benefits fail due to our error for a material part of the period, contact us — we may extend the period or credit the unused part.

## 5. Unused balance on account closure

- If you close your account voluntarily, unused **credits remain forfeited to the extent permitted by law**, except: (a) Stars-purchased unused Credits where you request a refund within 14 days of the purchase and it falls under §2; and (b) cases where the law of your country requires a refund.
- Balances obtained via referral/premium promotions have no cash value and are never refundable.

## 6. Fraud, abuse and violations

No refunds (and forfeiture of unused Credits to the extent permitted by law) where the account is terminated for breach of the Terms or Acceptable Use Policy, fraud, chargeback abuse, or payment-method misuse (e.g., stolen cards/stolen funds, which we must report).

## 7. How to request; processing

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with: account email, order number(s), amount, payment method, and a short description. We respond within **7 business days**. Approved refunds are processed within **14 business days** via the original payment method where possible (Stars via Telegram; crypto only where technically possible). We keep records of all refund decisions.

## 8. Statutory rights

If mandatory consumer law in your country gives you stronger rights than this policy (e.g., statutory withdrawal periods for digital services, Bangladesh Consumer Rights Protection Act protections), those rights prevail over this policy to the extent of any conflict. Where a mandatory withdrawal right applies and you have already consumed Credits, the value of consumed usage is deducted.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes apply prospectively with notice in the Service. The version at the time of your purchase governs that purchase.
