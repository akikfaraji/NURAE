# NURAE Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** [VERSION] · **Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Operator:** [REGISTERED NAME] ("NURAE", "we") · **Report abuse:** [ABUSE EMAIL]

This policy forms part of the Terms of Service and applies to every account holder, their bots, and anything they send through or create with the Service (including AI prompts, files, broadcasts and end-user-facing content).

## 1. Prohibited content and activities

You must not use NURAE (or allow your bots or end users to use it) to:

1. **Break the law** — anything illegal under applicable law (Bangladesh law, the law where you or your users are, or Telegram's rules), including facilitation of crimes.
2. **Harm children** — any sexualization of minors, grooming or child-unsafe content. Zero tolerance; immediate termination and reporting to authorities.
3. **Fraud and deception** — scams, phishing, fake giveaways, pyramid/Ponzi schemes, fake investment or crypto "signals", impersonation of people/brands, deceptive AI personas, misleading claims you can't honor.
4. **Spam** — unsolicited bulk messaging, scraping Telegram users to message them, ignoring opt-outs, buying lists, or any broadcast that violates Telegram's anti-spam rules. All broadcasts require recipient consent and an easy way to stop.
5. **Harassment and threats** — stalking, doxxing, hate speech, targeted abuse, sexual harassment, content inciting violence.
6. **Malware and platform attacks** — viruses, credential theft, phishing kits, botnets, exploiting or probing vulnerabilities of NURAE, Telegram or third parties, bypassing rate limits or billing.
7. **Privacy violations** — collecting or sharing personal data without a lawful basis, scraping private data, publishing others' private information.
8. **IP infringement** — using content you don't have rights to (pirated media, cloned paid products, trademark impersonation).
9. **Regulated activities without authorization** — gambling where prohibited or unlicensed; unlicensed medical, legal, financial or immigration advice; securities/token offerings; adult content where illegal.
10. **Abuse of the platform economics** — referral farming, fake accounts, multi-accounting to harvest free allowances/premium days, payment fraud, abuse of refunds.
11. **AI misuse** — generating content that violates this policy, attempting to bypass model safety systems, or presenting AI output as professional advice where it could cause harm.

## 2. AI-specific rules

- Bots answering with AI must not deceptively present themselves as human where that deception could mislead or harm.
- Do not use AI features to produce content prohibited in §1, including scaled deceptive content ("deep-fake"-style text/media).
- You remain responsible for everything your bot sends, whether an AI wrote it or not.
- You must comply with the acceptable-use terms of the AI provider your bot uses (BYOK owners included).

## 3. Limits

We apply technical limits (message rates, broadcast fan-out, file size/types, builder usage, top-up caps) to protect the platform. Circumventing limits — through multiple accounts, automation, or manufacturing usage — is a violation.

## 4. Enforcement

Severity-based, applied with an audit trail:

1. **Warning** — minor, likely unintentional issues; a fix is requested.
2. **Feature pause / limits** — bots or features paused while a violation is corrected (e.g., broadcasts disabled).
3. **Bot stopped / account suspended** — repeated or significant violations; hosting suspended.
4. **Termination** — serious violations (child safety, fraud, malware, attacks, illegal use): immediate, with unused Credits forfeited to the extent permitted by law, and reports to Telegram or authorities where we believe the law requires or permits it.

We may remove content, cooperate with Telegram, and preserve evidence for investigations. You can appeal any enforcement decision at [ABUSE EMAIL]; we aim to respond within 7 business days.
