# NURAE Privacy Policy

**Version:** [VERSION] · **Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE] · **Controller:** [REGISTERED NAME] ("NURAE", "we"), [BUSINESS ADDRESS], Bangladesh · **Contact:** [SUPPORT EMAIL]

This policy explains what personal data NURAE processes, why, and your rights. It covers the NURAE website, the assistant/agents, and the bot hosting platform.

## 1. Two roles we play (important)

- **Account data.** For your NURAE account, we are the **controller** — we decide how it is processed.
- **Your bots' end-user data.** When your Telegram bot's users interact with your bot, **you** (the bot owner) are the controller of that data; NURAE is a **processor** acting on your instructions. End users should first contact **you**. We offer a simple DPA text for business owners on request ([SUPPORT EMAIL]).

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | name, email, hashed password, verification status, Google sign-in ID, plan/entitlements | you |
| Bot & content | bot configs, commands, workflows, texts, templates, uploaded files (documents/images) | you |
| Assistant & agent usage | chat messages to the NURAE assistant, agent steps/audit trail, tool invocations | you (using the Service) |

We do **not** receive your card data (Telegram handles Stars payments) and we do not collect biometric or special-category data.

## 4. AI processing disclosure

Assistant and bot replies are generated by **third-party AI providers** (e.g., OpenRouter, OrcaRouter, DeepSeek, Zhipu/GLM, OpenAI — whichever is configured for that bot). To generate a reply, the relevant conversation content is sent to the selected provider. Provider data-handling rules differ: **free-tier models may log or use inputs to improve services**, while paid/BYOK providers typically do not train on API data. Do not submit secrets, regulated or sensitive personal data through AI features; for sensitive workloads, choose BYOK with a provider you trust and configure accordingly.

## 5. Sharing & sub-processors

We share data only as needed to run the Service, with:

- **Telegram** — bot API, Star payments, platform rules (independent controller for its own processing).
- **AI providers** — the selected model provider receives the content needed to answer (see §4).
- **Infrastructure sub-processors** — [HOSTING PROVIDER], [DATABASE PROVIDER], [EMAIL/SMTP PROVIDER], error monitoring/analytics as configured.
- **CryptoBot (Pay)** — if the automated crypto top-up rail is enabled.
- **Authorities** — when legally required, with review of validity.

Blockchain note: crypto transfers are public and permanent — tx hashes and addresses you submit are inherently public data.

## 6. International transfers

We are based in Bangladesh; our infrastructure may be hosted in the EU/US or elsewhere. Transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses with sub-processors where required) and on your instructions when you connect providers. You (bot owners) decide which AI provider receives your bots' conversations.

## 7. Retention

- Account & content: while your account is active; deleted (or anonymized) after account deletion, subject to §7 bullets below.
- Financial records (ledger, orders, receipts): kept as required for accounting/tax ([5] years) — ledger entries are pseudonymized on account deletion.
- Bot end-user data: controlled by you; deleted with the bot/conversation or on your instruction.
- Logs: short rolling window (typically ≤ 90 days) unless needed for an open investigation.
- Backups: overwritten on rotation (≤ [30] days).

## 8. Security

Encryption of secrets (bot tokens, provider keys) at rest, hashed passwords, least-privilege queries (ownership enforced at query level), admin audit trails, dependency updates, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure — report issues to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including GDPR-style rights where your regulator applies): **access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection**; withdraw consent; complain to your supervisory authority. We provide **self-service data export and account deletion** in the Service. End users of a customer's bot: contact the bot owner first; we will assist them as processor. Requests: [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we respond within 30 days.

## 10. Children

The Service is for users **18+**. Bots must not be directed at children; if your bot's audience includes minors, you (as controller) must comply with the applicable children's-data rules. If we learn an account belongs to a child, we will close it.

## 11. Cookies

We use only **essential cookies** (session/authentication, CSRF, rate-limiting). If we add analytics/marketing cookies, we will add a consent banner and update this policy first.
